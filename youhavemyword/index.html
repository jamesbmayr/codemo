Avoid outright sharing your words! Instead, hint at what you have so that only someone else with the same word would know what you mean.

Rules (1/3)

There is a conflict brewing.

The The Quorum of Blue is an underground resistance, while The Red Faction seeks to suppress all dissent.

But no one knows where their fellow citizens' loyalties lie.

Rules (2/3)

Each round, 3 words are transmitted to you.

If you are a Secret Member of the Quorum of Blue...

  • One of those is the code word. But which one?
  • Talk to your teammates to see which one word you all have in common.
  • But beware! The Red Faction will be posing as your allies, trying to trick you.

If you are a Loyalist Soldier-spy for the Red Faction...

  • All three words are decoys. None of these is the code word.
  • You also won't know who else is loyal to the Red Faction.
  • Talk to other citizens to determine the code word, and try to trick the Quorum of Blue into thinking it's one of your decoys.

Rules (3/3)

Either way, you'll need to talk to lots of people.

But talk in riddles; be vague, with just a hint of direction. You're trying to convey information while enemies are listening (or confuse your opponents while still seeming trustworthy).

  • The Quorum of Blue needs all members to enter the code word. If even a single member is wrong, the Red Faction succeeds instead.
  • The Red Faction can also succeed if one or more soldiers correctly identify the code word.
  • The conflict ends when one side achieves three victories.
